**RoN-Fix is now called Accord.** The same project and the same fixes under a new name: Accord, the unofficial patch
for Rise of Nations: Extended Edition. Your settings and your ban list stay, and the files in the game folder keep
their names (`ronfix.ini`, `ronfix-bans.txt`).

**Already have RoN-Fix?** Nothing to do: this version downloads itself while you play and is used from your next start.
The Start menu entry changes from RoN-Fix to Accord the first time you open it.

**New here?** Download **Accord-Setup.exe** below, close the game, double-click it and press **Install**.
If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**New in 0.16: see who has it**

- **Who in the lobby has Accord.** Some of what Accord does only works when everyone in the lobby has it, and until
  now nobody could tell. Your lobby chat now says, on your screen only, which players have Accord
  ("Bravo has Accord 0.16"), who plays without it, and sums the lobby up when the host starts. Nothing is written
  into the chat the others see, and players without Accord notice nothing. To make this possible your game tells
  the other players' Accord which Accord release you run, and nothing else. Switches: `Tags` and `TagLines` under
  `[lobby]` in ronfix.ini.
- **Leftovers of the old Better graphics option are removed.** If you ticked it in RoN-Fix 0.12 and have only ever
  updated automatically since, the files it left behind are taken out now.
- **Steam mirror (a test, off by default).** Accord players already ping each other over Steam's network, next to
  the game's own connection (PlayFab). With `Mirror=1` under `[steam]` in ronfix.ini, everything your game sends to
  another player who has it on too also travels over Steam, and Accord compares what arrives with what the game's
  own connection delivered: which was first, by how much, and whether anything was missing. The game itself still
  uses only its own connection. The results decide whether a later version carries the game between Accord players
  over Steam.
- **Gameplay fixes per match (for testers, off by default).** Groundwork for switching gameplay bug fixes on for a
  match when every player in the lobby has them ready, and playing the original game otherwise, without leaving the
  game. It needs a separate gameplay pack that is not part of this download, and does nothing without it.
- Players on RoN-Fix 0.8 to 0.15 are shown as having an older version; playing with them works as before.
