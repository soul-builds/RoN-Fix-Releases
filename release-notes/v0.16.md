**Already have RoN-Fix?** Nothing to do: this version downloads itself while you play and is used from your next start.

**New here?** Download **RoN-Fix-Setup.exe** below, close the game, double-click it and press **Install**.
If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

**New in 0.16: a test for carrying the game over Steam**

- **Nothing changes in your games.** The new part does nothing unless you switch it on.
- **Steam mirror (a test, off by default).** RoN-Fix players already ping each other over Steam's network, next to
  the game's own connection (PlayFab). With `Mirror=1` under `[steam]` in ronfix.ini, everything your game sends to
  another player who has it on too also travels over Steam, and RoN-Fix compares what arrives with what the game's
  own connection delivered: which was first, by how much, and whether anything was missing. The game itself still
  uses only its own connection. The results (lines MIRROR and WAIT in ronfix-net.log) decide whether a later
  version carries the game between RoN-Fix players over Steam.
- Players on RoN-Fix 0.8 to 0.15 are simply left out of the comparison; playing with them works as before.
